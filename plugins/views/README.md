# views

Shows buffers: a view is one buffer in one place, with its own cursors, scroll position, folds, narrowing and options, drawn as HTML. Every client shows a view at one place in its text, fitted to its own size. The browser scrolls a view natively over the items drawn near that place, with spacers for the rest, and draws its scroll bars. `place.js` measures the drawn items into each view's height table, sizes the spacers from it and places the first screen before the browser paints it, and shares those measures with `views.js`; `views.js` holds what is drawn still, rests the view after the hand's scroll and reports where the view is; `views.css` styles the text, cursors, region and scroll bars.
