# views

Shows buffers: a view is one buffer in one place, with its own cursors, scroll position, folds, narrowing and options, drawn as HTML. Every client shows a view at one place in its text, fitted to its own size. The browser scrolls a view natively over the items drawn near that place, with spacers for the rest. `views.js` holds what is drawn still, reports where the view is and draws its scroll bars from the text in view; `place.js` sizes the spacers and places the first screen before the browser paints it, and shares those measures with `views.js`; `views.css` styles the text, cursors, region and bars.
